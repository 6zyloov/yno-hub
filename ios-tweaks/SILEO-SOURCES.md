## Safety notes

- Do not add random “all tweaks” repos or recompiled forks just because they advertise compatibility.
- Never install two jailbreak-detection bypass tweaks together. Shadow's own guidance recommends disabling competing tweaks per app when troubleshooting.
- A repository being reachable does not mean every package in it supports your iOS version.
- App Store++ and old theming packages may be unavailable, discontinued or incompatible with newer iOS releases. Confirm in Sileo before installing.
- Avoid downloading paid packages from unofficial mirrors.
